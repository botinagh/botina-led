How it varies

        

        

        
The plan is made once and then fixed, so every take plays the same cues on the same beats. Re-roll a section until you like it, or pick any clip yourself. A show uses no microphone: the sound that drives the effects is simulated from your tempo and sections. Small random details inside a clip, like individual sparks, can still differ between takes.
